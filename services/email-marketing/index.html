What we do

Email Marketing

The one channel where the audience is already yours and the traffic is already paid for. Fully managed, monthly, run properly instead of occasionally.

What this covers

  1. Campaign strategy and send calendar

    What goes out, to whom and when, decided in advance rather than the week of.

  2. Lifecycle flows

    Welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post purchase and winback. The sequences that run whether or not anyone is at a desk.

  3. Email design and copy

    Both, by the same team, so the words and the layout are not arguing with each other.

  4. Segmentation

    Different messages for people at different stages, instead of one send to everybody.

  5. Pop ups and list growth

    Capturing the traffic already arriving, before it leaves and never comes back.

  6. Deliverability and domain health

    Authentication, reputation and list hygiene, so the work actually reaches an inbox.

  7. A and B testing and reporting

    Subject lines, offers and timing tested, with the result carried into the next send.

The list is the only audience a platform cannot take away from you.
